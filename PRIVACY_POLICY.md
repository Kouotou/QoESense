# QoESense — Privacy Policy & Informed Consent

**This document mirrors the in-app consent screen** (`ui/onboarding/ConsentScreen.kt`) shown
before any permission is requested or any data is collected. Keep the two in sync if either
changes.

Last updated: 2026-08-28

## About this study

QoESense is part of an MSc research project studying how network signal conditions affect
real-world app experience, so that quality of experience can eventually be predicted directly
from network data. Participation is completely voluntary: you can stop at any time by
disabling collection in the app or uninstalling it, with no consequences.

## What we collect

- Network signal metrics: signal strength and quality (RSRP, RSRQ, SINR), timing advance,
  network type (LTE), a one-way hashed cell tower identifier (not the real ID), and, on
  some devices, additional channel details.
- Connection info: Wi-Fi vs. cellular, roaming status, an estimate of throughput and latency.
- Approximate location, sampled alongside each network reading, to map coverage along your
  usual routes.
- Which app is in the foreground, checked periodically - used only to detect whether a
  social-media app or a video call is active, so we can tag the network conditions during that
  kind of use. We never see what you typed, sent, or viewed inside any app.
- Your device model, Android version, and the app's own version number.
- Your occasional 1-5 star ratings of network quality, when you respond to a prompt.

## What we never collect

Message content, call content, browsing history, photos, contacts, keystrokes, or anything
typed into any app - this app has no access to and does not request any of that.

## How your data is identified

You are never asked for your name, phone number, or email. Each install gets a random
anonymous ID generated by the app, and all your data is tied only to that ID - not to you
personally. We have no way to look up who a given ID belongs to from the data alone. Your
anonymous ID is shown on the app's Status screen if you ever need to reference it.

## Where your data goes and how it's protected

Data is uploaded only over Wi-Fi (never using your mobile data), encrypted in transit, and
stored on Google Firebase/Cloud infrastructure with access restricted to the research team.
Raw signal data is queued locally on your device and only deleted once a successful upload is
confirmed.

## How your data is used

Your data will be used to build and evaluate multiple deep learning models for predicting network
quality of experience, as part of an MSc research project. Aggregated or de-identified results
may be published in academic work; individual raw data will not be shared publicly or with
third parties.

## Special permissions this app asks for, and why

- **Location (foreground and background)**: required by Android itself to return any cell
  signal information, and used to map network coverage along your routes.
- **Usage Access**: lets the app detect which app is in the foreground, for the purposes
  described above - not to monitor your activity for any other reason.
- **Display over other apps**: lets the occasional quality-rating prompt appear right away, so
  your answer stays close in time to the network conditions it's about, rather than needing
  you to remember to open the app later.
- **Notifications**: used only for a low-priority "collection active" status indicator and the
  quality-rating prompts.
- **Battery optimization exemption**: needed so the app can keep collecting reliably over the
  weeks-long study, since Android aggressively suspends background apps otherwise.

## Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You can stop data collection at any time from the app's Status
screen, or withdraw completely by uninstalling the app - either way, no further data will be
collected from your device. To request that already-uploaded data tied to your device be
deleted, contact bilalkouotou@gmail.com with your anonymous device ID from the Status
screen.
